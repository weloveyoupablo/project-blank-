MEDIA · LONG FORM
The social feed should be infrastructure, not a collection of apps
AK
Amara King@amaraking · 2h
The internet taught people to divide expression by format. Text went one place, photographs another, short video somewhere else, and long video somewhere else again.

That structure made sense when each medium needed its own technology. It makes less sense when the same phone can create all of them instantly.

A modern social feed should not begin by asking what kind of media you are making. It should begin with a blank space and let the act of creation define the format. The interface can then adapt around what you made.

That is the point of one stream: not to flatten every medium into the same card, but to let every medium keep its own strengths while removing the walls between them.
Discussion, reposts and quote reposts stay attached to the title card on Home.
